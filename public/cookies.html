This Cookie Policy explains how Shradhalu Private Limited, operating as Sri Dwar, uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, mobile applications, and digital platforms.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our Platform. They help improve website functionality, remember preferences, analyze usage, and enhance security.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

  • User authentication
  • Security
  • Session management
  • Shopping cart and booking functionality

These are necessary for the Platform to operate.

Performance & Analytics Cookies These help us understand:

  • Visitor traffic
  • Popular pages
  • Device types
  • Website performance
  • Error reports

We currently use Google Analytics (GA4), delivered via Google Tag Manager, and Microsoft Clarity for this purpose. We do not currently use Firebase Analytics or any advertising/retargeting cookies.

Preference Cookies These remember:

  • Language
  • Region
  • User settings
  • Accessibility preferences

Marketing Cookies Where permitted by law, we may use cookies to:

  • Measure advertising performance
  • Personalize promotions
  • Prevent repetitive advertisements

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by trusted third-party providers including payment gateways, analytics providers, cloud hosting services, customer support tools, embedded video providers, or social media integrations.

Sri Dwar does not control third-party cookie policies.

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies
  • Restrict third-party cookies

Disabling cookies may affect certain Platform features.

5. Changes

Sri Dwar may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the latest version.

6. Contact

Questions regarding cookies may be submitted through the official contact details published on the Sri Dwar Platform.
