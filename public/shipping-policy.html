Shipping Policy

How Sri Dwar packages, dispatches, and delivers Prasad and other physical devotional items booked through the Platform.

This Shipping Policy explains how Shradhalu Private Limited, operating as Sri Dwar ("Sri Dwar", "we", "our", or "us"), handles the packaging, dispatch, and delivery of Prasad, sacred items, temple offerings, holy water, Vibhuti, Kumkum, Rudraksha, Tulsi, Yantras, certificates, and other physical devotional materials booked through the Platform.

This Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and Refund & Cancellation Policy.

1. What We Ship

Where a booked puja, seva, or contribution includes a physical component, Sri Dwar coordinates with the relevant temple, priest, or logistics partner to package and dispatch items such as Prasad, sacred ash (Vibhuti), Kumkum, Rudraksha, Tulsi, Yantras, religious literature, printed certificates, and similar devotional materials.

Packaging, quantity, and appearance may vary between temples based on their own local practices; certain items may be substituted where a specific offering is temporarily unavailable.

2. Delivery Countries

Physical Prasad and devotional item delivery is primarily offered within India through our registered courier and logistics partners. Delivery to addresses outside India may be offered on select temples/services, subject to destination-country customs regulations, import restrictions on religious or perishable items, and courier availability at the time of booking.

Where international shipping is not available for a particular item or destination, this will be indicated at the time of booking, or our support team will inform you before confirming the order.

3. Delivery Timelines

Estimated delivery timelines are communicated at the time of booking and are indicative only. Typical dispatch begins after the puja/seva is performed and Prasad is prepared and packaged by the temple or priest.

Actual delivery time depends on the courier partner, destination location, festival-season courier volumes, weather, and local conditions, and may vary between metro cities, smaller towns, and international addresses.

4. Customs, Duties & Import Restrictions (International Orders)

For deliveries outside India, the recipient is solely responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, clearance formalities, or restrictions imposed by the destination country. Some countries restrict or prohibit the import of religious items, organic materials (such as flowers, sweets, or ash), or liquids (such as holy water) — Sri Dwar is not responsible for shipments delayed, held, or destroyed by customs authorities for this reason.

We recommend international recipients check their country's customs rules for religious/devotional items before placing an order.

5. Lost Parcels

If a shipment does not arrive within a reasonable period beyond the estimated delivery window, please contact our support team with your Booking ID and tracking details (where available). We will coordinate with the courier partner to trace the shipment.

Where a parcel is confirmed lost in transit due to the courier partner's fault, Sri Dwar will, at its discretion, arrange a reshipment where feasible or an appropriate resolution in line with our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Sri Dwar is not responsible for loss caused by an incorrect or incomplete address supplied by the user, or by a recipient's failure to collect the parcel from the courier.

6. Damaged Parcels

If your parcel arrives visibly damaged, or the contents are damaged, please notify our support team within 48 hours of delivery, along with photographs of the packaging and item(s), and your Booking ID. Sri Dwar may, at its discretion, offer a replacement, reshipment, or an appropriate refund where the damage is attributable to packaging or handling within our reasonable control.

Perishable offerings (such as Prasad, flowers, or fruit) are naturally subject to some deterioration during transit; this alone does not indicate mishandling.

7. Undelivered / Refused Shipments

Shipments that cannot be delivered due to an incorrect or incomplete address, an unavailable recipient after reasonable delivery attempts, refusal to accept delivery, or customs rejection, may not qualify for a free reshipment or refund, except where required by applicable law.

8. Tracking & Support

Where a tracking number is available, it will be shared with you via email, SMS, or WhatsApp once your item is dispatched. For any shipping query, contact us with your Booking ID at puja@sridwar.com or WhatsApp +91 97776 45062.

9. Changes to this Policy

Sri Dwar may update this Shipping Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our logistics partners, service areas, or operational practices. The revised version becomes effective upon publication on the Platform.
