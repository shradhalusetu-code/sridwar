This Refund & Cancellation Policy ("Policy") forms an integral part of the Terms & Conditions governing the use of Sri Dwar, operated by Shradhalu Private Limited ("Sri Dwar," "we," "our," or "us").

By booking any service, making any payment, or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Policy.

1. Purpose

Sri Dwar provides a technology platform facilitating religious, spiritual, devotional, charitable, pilgrimage, and related services through temples, priests, trusts, charitable institutions, logistics providers, and other independent partners.

Many of these services involve advance arrangements, reservations, contributions, temple scheduling, procurement of ritual materials, priest allocation, travel coordination, and other time-sensitive preparations. Accordingly, refunds and cancellations depend upon the nature of the service and the stage at which cancellation occurs.

2. Definitions

For the purposes of this Policy:

Booking means any confirmed request for a service through Sri Dwar.

Service includes pujas, rituals, contributions, livestreams, astrology consultations, pilgrimage services, prasad delivery, temple bookings, charitable activities, and any other offerings available on the Platform.

Platform Fee means the service fee charged by Sri Dwar for facilitating bookings and related services.

Third-Party Charges include amounts payable to temples, priests, trusts, logistics providers, payment gateways, travel operators, accommodation providers, vendors, or other independent service providers.

Force Majeure has the meaning assigned in the Terms & Conditions.

3. Nature of Religious Services

Many religious services begin preparation immediately after booking confirmation. Such preparations may include:

  • reserving temple slots
  • scheduling priests
  • purchasing flowers, fruits, milk, ghee, cloth, and puja materials
  • arranging livestreams
  • preparing Prasad
  • coordinating logistics
  • making contributions to temples or trusts

Once such preparations commence, recovery of amounts paid to independent service providers may not always be possible.

4. Dynamic Pricing

Service prices are not fixed and may vary due to:

  • festival seasons
  • auspicious dates
  • temple crowd
  • priest availability
  • local customs
  • transportation costs
  • government regulations
  • material costs
  • inflation
  • operational requirements
  • other circumstances beyond reasonable control

Price variations after booking confirmation do not automatically entitle a User to cancellation or refund.

5. Cancellation by User

Users may request cancellation through the official Sri Dwar support channels.

Cancellation requests become effective only after written confirmation from Sri Dwar.

The following general principles apply unless otherwise specified for a particular service:

Before Processing Begins

Where no arrangements have been initiated and no payments have been transferred to third parties, Sri Dwar may approve cancellation subject to deduction of:

  • payment gateway charges
  • taxes
  • administrative expenses
  • applicable Platform Fees

After Processing Begins

Where booking processing has commenced, Sri Dwar may deduct:

  • Platform Fees
  • payment gateway charges
  • taxes
  • actual expenses incurred
  • non-refundable third-party charges

After Service Commencement

Once a ritual, consultation, livestream, pilgrimage, contribution process, or other booked service has commenced, the booking shall generally be considered fulfilled and no refund shall ordinarily be available except where required by applicable law.

6. Cancellation by Sri Dwar

Sri Dwar may cancel or reschedule bookings due to:

  • temple closure
  • priest unavailability
  • natural disasters
  • security concerns
  • government restrictions
  • operational limitations
  • technical failures
  • force majeure
  • fraud prevention
  • incomplete booking information
  • legal requirements

Where feasible, Sri Dwar may offer:

  • rescheduling
  • an alternative service
  • partial refund
  • full refund of recoverable amounts

7. Temple and Priest Cancellations

Temples and priests operate independently.

If a temple, priest, trust, or other independent service provider cancels, postpones, or modifies a service, Sri Dwar will make reasonable efforts to:

  • arrange another priest
  • coordinate another temple
  • reschedule the service
  • provide alternative arrangements where practicable

Refunds, if any, shall depend upon the recoverability of payments already made to independent service providers.

8. Contributions

Contributions made to:

  • temples
  • trusts
  • gaushalas
  • charitable institutions
  • annadanam programmes
  • religious causes
  • are generally voluntary

Once transferred to the designated recipient, such contributions are ordinarily non-refundable.

Where a contribution has not yet been transferred, Sri Dwar may review cancellation requests on a case-by-case basis.

Nothing in this section limits any mandatory rights available under applicable law.

9. Prasad and Physical Deliveries

Refunds shall generally not be available for:

  • delivery delays caused by courier partners
  • customs delays
  • weather disruptions
  • recipient unavailability
  • incorrect delivery information supplied by the User

Where products arrive materially damaged due to packaging attributable to Sri Dwar, users should notify customer support within forty-eight (48) hours of delivery with reasonable supporting evidence.

Sri Dwar may, at its discretion, offer replacement, reshipment, or an appropriate refund where justified.

10. Livestream Services

Temporary interruptions resulting from:

  • internet failures
  • power outages
  • temple restrictions
  • weather
  • equipment malfunction
  • local infrastructure limitations
  • shall not automatically qualify for refunds

Where a livestream cannot be provided due solely to circumstances attributable to Sri Dwar and no reasonable alternative is offered, Sri Dwar may consider an appropriate refund or service credit.

11. Pilgrimage, Travel and Accommodation

Travel, accommodation, transportation, and pilgrimage services may be subject to the cancellation policies of independent providers.

Airlines, railways, hotels, taxi operators, tour operators, and accommodation providers may impose separate cancellation charges.

Sri Dwar shall not be responsible for charges imposed by such independent providers.

12. Astrology and Consultation Services

Once an astrology consultation, spiritual consultation, or similar appointment has commenced, the service shall generally be considered completed.

Refunds shall not ordinarily be granted based solely upon dissatisfaction with advice, predictions, interpretations, or subjective expectations.

13. Failed Payments

Where payment fails but funds are debited by a bank or payment provider, reversal timelines shall be governed by the relevant financial institution.

Sri Dwar shall reasonably cooperate in resolving such matters but does not control banking settlement timelines.

14. Fraudulent Transactions

Sri Dwar reserves the right to refuse refunds where there is reasonable evidence of:

  • payment fraud
  • identity theft
  • abuse of refund policies
  • chargeback misuse
  • false claims
  • duplicate refund requests

Appropriate legal action may be initiated where necessary.

15. Refund Method

Approved refunds shall ordinarily be processed through the original payment method used for the transaction, unless otherwise required by law or operational constraints.

Where the original payment method is unavailable, Sri Dwar may use another lawful payment mechanism after appropriate verification.

16. Refund Processing Time

After approval, refunds are generally initiated within 7 to 15 business days.

Actual credit timelines depend upon:

  • banks
  • card networks
  • UPI providers
  • payment gateways
  • financial institutions

Sri Dwar is not responsible for delays attributable to these independent entities.

17. Force Majeure

Refund obligations may be affected by Force Majeure events including:

  • floods
  • earthquakes
  • pandemics
  • riots
  • war
  • government restrictions
  • transportation failures
  • internet outages
  • temple closures
  • natural disasters
  • public emergencies

Sri Dwar shall make commercially reasonable efforts to provide rescheduling or alternative arrangements where feasible.

18. Customer Support

Users requiring cancellation, modification, refund assistance, or clarification should contact Sri Dwar through the official communication channels published on the Platform.

Users should provide:

  • Booking ID
  • registered mobile number or email
  • payment reference
  • reason for the request
  • supporting documents where applicable

Providing complete information helps expedite processing.

19. Changes to this Policy

Sri Dwar reserves the right to amend or update this Policy from time to time in response to legal, operational, technological, or business developments.

The revised version shall become effective upon publication on the Platform unless otherwise specified.

20. Final Provisions

This Policy shall be read together with the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Legal Disclaimer.

Nothing contained in this Policy excludes or limits any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

Where a conflict exists between this Policy and mandatory legal requirements, the applicable law shall prevail to the extent of such conflict.
