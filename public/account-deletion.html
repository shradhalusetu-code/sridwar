What gets deleted

When you submit a verified deletion request, we permanently delete or anonymize:

  • Your Dharmic ID login (name, email, encrypted password)
  • Your saved devotee profile (Gotra, Nakshatra, saved addresses)
  • Your booking preferences and saved contact details

We may keep limited records — such as completed payment/transaction references — for a limited period where Indian law requires it (e.g. tax and accounting rules), or to resolve an open dispute, refund, or fraud investigation already in progress. These are deleted once that legal or operational need ends. See our Privacy Policy — Account & Data Deletion section for full details.
