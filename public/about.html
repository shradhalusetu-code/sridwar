Sri Dwar is a devotional-services platform, operated by Shradhalu Private Limited, that connects devotees around the world to temples across India — offering puja bookings, seva sponsorships, live darshan streaming, Prasad delivery, astrology consultations, and a temple/pujari directory.

Our Mission

"We didn't build Sri Dwar to replace physical holy temple visits, but to bridge the spatial divide for elderly parents, sick individuals, and global NRIs — and to provide sustainable, transparent financial conduits for traditional priests, local flower vendors, and indigenous cows."

Why Sri Dwar Exists

Millions of devotees are separated from their family temple by distance, age, illness, or circumstance. Sri Dwar exists to close that gap — pairing dependable technology with a deep respect for Vedic tradition, so that a Sankalpa performed on your behalf, thousands of miles away, feels as real as standing at the sanctum door yourself.

At the same time, we aim to build a sustainable, transparent channel of support for the priests, temple staff, and local communities who keep these traditions alive.

Our Vision

Faith Beyond Distance. Blessings Beyond Borders. We want every devotee, wherever they live, to be able to stay meaningfully connected to their temple traditions — with clarity about what is being booked, who is performing it, and how their contribution is used.
