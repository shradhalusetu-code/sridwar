Sri Dwar aims to provide a respectful, inclusive, and spiritually enriching environment for devotees, temples, priests, partners, and visitors.

1. Respect

Users shall treat everyone with dignity and respect regardless of religion, caste, gender, nationality, language, or background.

2. Authentic Information

Users should provide truthful booking details, contact information, and service requests.

3. Appropriate Behaviour

Users shall not:

  • abuse priests or temple staff
  • harass other users
  • use offensive language
  • discriminate
  • threaten anyone
  • spread misinformation
  • impersonate others
  • engage in unlawful conduct

4. Religious Respect

Users shall respect:

  • temple customs
  • local traditions
  • dress codes
  • photography restrictions
  • ceremonial procedures
  • instructions issued by temple authorities

5. Reviews

Reviews should be:

  • honest
  • respectful
  • relevant
  • based on genuine experiences

Fake, misleading, defamatory, or manipulated reviews are prohibited.

6. Safety

Users shall comply with safety instructions issued by temple authorities, local administration, or Sri Dwar.

7. Privacy

Users shall not publish another person's personal information without lawful authority.

8. Platform Integrity

Users shall not:

  • attempt unauthorized access
  • distribute malware
  • misuse contributions
  • manipulate bookings
  • interfere with Platform operations

9. Enforcement

Sri Dwar may remove content, suspend accounts, cancel bookings, or terminate access where these Guidelines are violated.

10. Reporting

Users are encouraged to report misconduct, fraud, abuse, or suspicious activity through official Sri Dwar support channels.
