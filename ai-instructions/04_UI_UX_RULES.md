# SriDwar — UI/UX Rules

## Core Goal
The experience should feel reliable, polished, trustworthy and easy to understand.

## Responsive Design
Cards, sections, forms and dialogs must adapt cleanly across supported screen sizes.

Within a single carousel/section:
- cards should have consistent height
- card width may remain responsive
- longer content must not unexpectedly create a much taller card
- do not hide important information merely to force equal sizing

## Layout Quality
Avoid:
- excessive empty space
- overlapping content
- cut-off text/buttons
- horizontal overflow
- broken media containers
- inconsistent spacing
- stretched/squeezed images
- unusable dialogs
- buttons merging with prices
- hidden controls behind device/system areas

## Content Density
Use concise initial views where long descriptions are unnecessary.
Prefer:
- concise title/summary
- Read More / expansion for full details

## Payments
Payment UI must be especially clear and trustworthy. Never present a broken payment method as available.

## Disclaimers
Do not repeat the same disclaimer at every step when it is only required at a specific step. Preserve checkbox/functionality/legal meaning while presenting wording naturally.

## Accessibility & Usability
Maintain readable typography, sufficient contrast, clear controls, logical focus/order and usable touch targets.
