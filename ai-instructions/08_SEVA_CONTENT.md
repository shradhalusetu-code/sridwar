# SriDwar — Seva, Puja & Service Content Rules

## Content Principles
Service information should be:
- accurate
- concise
- devotional
- respectful
- easy to understand

## Cards
If a section title already states a common starting price, avoid redundantly repeating the same price on every card unless needed for clarity.

## Expandable Content
Use concise initial card/list content and allow users to open/read more when long descriptions are necessary.

## Service-Specific Information
Do not force every service into identical generic wording. Show the relevant:
- service name
- significance/purpose
- what is included
- what the devotee receives
- applicable timing/availability information
- payment information where applicable

## Disclaimers
Preserve required safeguards while using gentle, welcoming language.
Do not change legal meaning or checkbox logic merely while improving wording.

## Pricing
When a pricing discrepancy is discovered, verify the actual current source and calculation before changing values. Do not guess.
