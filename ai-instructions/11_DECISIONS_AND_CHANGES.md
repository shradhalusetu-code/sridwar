## Current Decisions

### Documents
Acknowledgement certificates are separate JPG/PNG assets.
Confirmation/information PDFs remain separate.
Final personalized completion certificates are prepared after actual service completion.

### Payment
Do not equate a database payment row with confirmed payment.
Final payment confirmation requires valid confirmation state and duplicate-send protection.

### UX
Preserve working designs and make the smallest necessary change.
Use concise lists/cards with expansion for long information where appropriate.

### Disclaimers
Improve wording without changing required legal meaning, checkbox requirements or functionality.
