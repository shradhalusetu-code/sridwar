# SriDwar — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Character
SriDwar should feel:
- devotional
- humble
- trustworthy
- respectful
- warm
- culturally rooted
- professional
- calm
- welcoming

## Visual Direction
Use the established SriDwar palm-leaf visual language where applicable:
- authentic aged/dried palm-leaf texture
- natural grain/fibres/cracks
- irregular/rugged leaf edges
- layered physical depth
- binding holes and rope/thread details
- ornamental borders
- tasteful Hindu/Dharmic motifs
- temple/deity artwork
- deepam/diya lighting
- natural shadows and highlights
- SriDwar logo/watermark
- Sanskrit/devotional writing where appropriate
- QR/barcode/social elements where required

Do not flatten this into generic brown paper, white cards, pasted artwork or unrelated modern decoration.

## Content Tone
Prefer simple, devotional, subtle, humble and welcoming language.

Avoid:
- harsh legalistic language
- fear-based wording
- unnecessary warnings
- defensive wording
- exaggerated claims

Do not weaken legally/operationally necessary safeguards.

## Branding Consistency
Do not invent new logos, slogans, symbols or brand systems when an existing project asset already provides the correct source.
